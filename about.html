About

I'm Arkoday — a third-year BSc Computer Science student at the University of York, based between Coventry and York.

I work mainly in Python, in machine learning and data science. What I'm most drawn to is biological and "organic"-inspired artificial intelligence, and I chose my degree partly to be able to go further in that direction.

Most recently I spent four months as a data science intern at Bluevia Health, working on venue-of-care analysis towards interpretable risk stratification — building patient cohorts, enriching them with medication and lab data, and running statistical analyses on outcomes like length of stay and readmission, all under patient privacy constraints. It was my first extended experience of working with real, messy, sensitive data rather than a clean dataset, and most of what I learned was about the preparation rather than the modelling.
